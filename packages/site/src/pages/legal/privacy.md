This Privacy Policy explains how Sad Coder, Inc. (Sad Coder, we, us, or our) handles personal data
when you use Tidemarks, including the reader at app.tidemarks.io, the syncing service behind it, and
its MCP server (collectively, the Service).

Sad Coder is the controller of personal data described in this policy unless we state otherwise. Our
business and privacy contact details are:

Sad Coder, Inc., 5F., No. 50, Zhongyang 7th St., Xindian Dist., New Taipei City 231014, Taiwan —
[contact@tidemarks.io](mailto:contact@tidemarks.io)

This policy does not govern a third party's independent processing, including Paddle's transaction
processing or an agent or client you choose to connect.

## The short version

**Without an account, we hold nothing.** Tidemarks reads epub files in your browser. The books you
import, the highlights and notes you write, and where you are in each book are stored on your own
device and are never sent to us. There is no analytics script, no advertising, and no account to
create.

**With an account, we hold only the books that are syncing.** A free account syncs three books; a
paid plan removes that limit. A book is either entirely on the server or not on it at all — there is
no halfway state where we have the title but not the file. Every other book on your device stays
there, and we never learn it exists.

## What we hold when you have an account

**Your account.** Your email address, and the passkeys registered to the account. We use these to
let you in and to reach you about the Service. A sign-in code is short-lived and can be used once;
we keep a record that it was sent, with the address it went to, because that record is how we limit
how often codes can be requested. Those records are cleared once they are older than that limit.

**The books that are syncing.** For each one: the epub file exactly as you imported it, its cover,
its title and author, your reading position, your highlights and notes, and your reading sessions —
the times at which you read it, which the reader uses to estimate how long a chapter will take you.

**Your subscription state.** Whether the account is paid, and an identifier that lets us open
Paddle's customer portal for you. We never see your card. Paddle collects and holds payment details
as merchant of record; see [Paddle's privacy notice](https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy).

**Ordinary server logs**, kept briefly for security and debugging.

## What we do not do

These are commitments, not current settings:

- **We do not scan the contents of your books**, and nothing about what you read feeds a
  recommendation, a profile, or a model.
- **We do not deduplicate.** Two people who import the same book have two separate copies on the
  server, byte for byte. Your copy is yours.
- **We never publish a link to a book.** Every file is streamed through a request that checks it is
  yours first; there are no signed URLs and no public addresses.
- **We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.**
- **We use no third-party analytics and set no advertising cookies.**

## Why we are allowed to hold it

Where the GDPR or a similar law applies, we rely on: **performance of a contract** for running your
account and syncing your books; **legitimate interests** for keeping the Service secure and working;
and **legal obligation** where a law requires us to keep something. Where consent is required, we
ask for it and you can withdraw it.

## Who else sees it

Only the processors it takes to run the Service:

- **Cloudflare**, which hosts the Service and stores the database and files.
- **Resend**, which delivers sign-in and service emails.
- **Paddle**, as merchant of record for payments. Paddle is an independent controller of the
  transaction data it collects.

We do not send your books, highlights or notes to any of them beyond what hosting requires, and we
share personal data with a public authority only where the law requires it.

## An agent you connect

Tidemarks has an MCP server, so an AI agent you authorize can read your books, notes and reading
positions. **It sees only the books that are syncing**, never one that is only on a device, and
never a frozen one. What that agent then does with what it reads is governed by whoever operates it,
not by this policy. You can revoke access by removing the authorization.

## How long we keep it

**Your account and its syncing books** are kept while the account exists.

**A frozen book** — one above your sync limit after a subscription ends — is kept for **at least 24
months**. The copy on your own devices is untouched by this, and always has been.

**A book you delete** stops syncing as soon as the deletion reaches us, and an agent can no longer
read it. Its file is removed from our storage when we next sweep for orphaned files; we do not
promise that this happens the same day. If you want a book gone from the server now, write to us.

**Sign-in records and the sign-up list** hold email addresses, as described above, and ordinary
server logs are kept briefly for security and debugging.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object
to our processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to receive your data in a portable
form. To exercise any of these, write to
[contact@tidemarks.io](mailto:contact@tidemarks.io); we may need to verify that you control the
account's email address.

Two of these you can do without asking us. **Export** is built into the reader: the account pane
writes your books, notes and reading positions to a file you keep, and the epub in it is the file you
imported, unchanged. It writes the books this device has the file for — a book that is syncing but
has not been opened on this device yet needs opening once first. **Deleting a book** in the reader
removes it from the server as well, on the terms in the retention section above.

To close an account entirely, write to us and we will delete it and everything the server holds for
it. There is no self-service button for this yet.

You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children

The Service is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If
you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

## International transfers

The Service runs on Cloudflare's network, so data may be processed in a country other than yours.
Where a transfer needs a safeguard under the GDPR or a similar law, we rely on the appropriate
mechanism, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Security

Books and covers are streamed only after the request is checked against the account that owns them.
Sign-in uses passkeys or a one-time code sent to your email address, so control of that mailbox is
control of the account — keep it secure. No service can promise perfect security, and we do not.

## Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version and update the date above, and for a
material change we will give reasonable notice through the Service or by email.
