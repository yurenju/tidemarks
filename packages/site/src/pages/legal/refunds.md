Tidemarks has one paid plan: US$20 a year, billed annually. This page says what happens when you
want that money back.

## Fourteen days, no questions

Within **14 days** of a payment, write to
[contact@tidemarks.io](mailto:contact@tidemarks.io) and ask for it back. We will not ask why. The
refund is issued through Paddle to the payment method you used, and your subscription is cancelled
at the same time.

The 14 days run from the date of the payment, which means a renewal has its own 14 days — you are
not held to a decision you made a year earlier.

## After fourteen days

We do not refund a part-used year. You can cancel at any time from Paddle's customer portal;
cancelling stops the next renewal and leaves the year you have paid for running until it ends.

This does not affect any statutory right you have that cannot lawfully be excluded. If consumer law
where you live gives you a stronger right than this page does, that law applies.

## What cancelling changes

Nothing on your own devices. Your books, notes and reading positions stay where they are, and
Tidemarks keeps working as a reader without an account.

On the server, the account's sync limit returns to three books: the three you have read most
recently keep syncing, and the rest are frozen. A frozen book is not deleted — our copy is kept for
24 months, it stops accepting changes, and a connected agent can no longer read it. Resubscribe
within that time and those books come back with whatever you did on each device while they were
frozen. See the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy) for what is stored and for how long.

Before you cancel, you can also export everything from the account pane in the reader. The epub in
that export is the file you imported, unchanged.

## Who to ask for what

**Refunds, invoices, receipts, tax questions and chargebacks** are Paddle's, because Paddle is the
merchant of record for every purchase — see the [terms of service](/legal/terms). Paddle's support
is at [paddle.net](https://paddle.net/), and Paddle's own refund policy applies to the transaction.

**Anything about Tidemarks itself** is ours:
[contact@tidemarks.io](mailto:contact@tidemarks.io). If you are not sure which it is, write to us
and we will sort it out.
